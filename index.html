Your privacy is a design principle, not a disclaimer.

Your information will not be stored at all. This is 100% anonymous and private. Your answers never leave your device — there is no server, no database, no analytics, no tracking pixels. Everything runs entirely in your browser. You can verify this by viewing the page source.

How this works

❶

Identify your district

We'll look up which races are actually on your ballot. No address required — you can do this manually with a link we provide.

❷

Answer questions about your values

Not a political quiz. Questions are designed to surface your real priorities — including ones you might not think to name — through concrete trade-offs, not political buzzwords. ~16–22 questions total.

❸

Get evidence-based results

Every recommendation is sourced. Every score shows its reasoning. AI was used to distill publicly available research — not to form opinions. You're seeing documented records matched against your stated priorities.

❹

Save and restore anytime

Download your answers as a JSON file to pick up where you left off. On desktop browsers, you can save directly. Mobile works too — a desktop browser gives the best experience.

A note on how AI is used here

This guide uses AI to distill publicly available information — voting records, campaign finance filings, news coverage, candidate statements — into a structured scoring matrix. AI is a research tool here, not an opinion-giver. The scoring methodology is fully transparent: every dimension and every score can be read in the page source. If you disagree with how something is scored, you should — and you can weight it out entirely.
